### 1. Executive Summary and Overall Supplier Rating
Global Bearings Ltd. has been an established tier-1 supplier of specialized high-load spherical and cylindrical roller bearings for Halvorsen Antriebstechnik GmbH since 2019. The components supplied are predominantly integrated into heavy planetary gearboxes produced at the Linz manufacturing plant.

During the 2024 calendar year, Global Bearings Ltd. achieved an overall weighted supplier performance score of **94.2 out of 100 points**. Under the internal Halvorsen Supplier Qualification Framework (HAT-SQF-Rev4), this performance tier confirms their classification as a **Grade A Preferred Supplier**.

### 3. Detailed Performance Metric Breakdown

#### A. Quality Performance (Weight: 40% — Score: 95.0 / 100)
- **Total Parts Received:** 14,280 bearing units across 18 call-off orders.
- **Defective Parts Inbound (PPM):** 210 PPM (parts per million), comfortably within the contractual threshold of < 300 PPM.
- **Non-Conformance Reports (NCR):** Only two minor NCRs were raised in 2024. Both involved slight packaging seal degradations caused during sea-freight handling; zero bearing functional defects were recorded on the Linz assembly lines.
- **Dimensional Stability:** Cpk values for bearing inner ring tolerances consistently exceeded 1.67 across all sampled batches.

#### B. Delivery Reliability and Lead Times (Weight: 35% — Score: 93.5 / 100)
- **On-Time In-Full (OTIF) Delivery Rate:** 98.1% of all shipment consignments arrived exactly within the agreed delivery window.
- **Agreed Standard Lead Time:** The contractually guaranteed lead time for standard catalog bearings is 14 business days from purchase order acknowledgement to factory gate delivery in Linz.
- **Safety Stocking Agreement:** Global Bearings maintains a buffer stock of 450 units of high-runner bearing HA-90215-K in their Rotterdam distribution warehouse, successfully preventing supply disruptions during European port strikes.

#### C. Commercial and Environmental Compliance (Weight: 25% — Score: 94.0 / 100)
- **Pricing Stability:** High compliance with agreed index pricing for steel raw materials.
- **Sustainability & ESG:** The Sheffield production site operates on 100% certified renewable energy and achieved ISO 14001:2015 recertification in October 2024.

### 4. Continuous Improvement Recommendations (Action Items)
1. Implement direct electronic data interchange (EDI) via ANSI ASC X12 / EDIFACT for automated delivery dispatch notices (DESADV) by Q3 2025.
2. Optimize anti-corrosive VCI foil packaging for maritime shipments to eliminate residual moisture risks during humid seasons.
3. Conduct joint value-engineering workshop in Sheffield during May 2025 to explore weight-optimized ceramic hybrid bearings for next-generation electric drivetrains.
